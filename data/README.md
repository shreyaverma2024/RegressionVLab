# Offline regression datasets

The two CSVs are local copies of public tutorial datasets retrieved on
2026-11-03. Numeric values, column names, and row order are preserved. These
are published teaching examples; their original collection methodology is
not established by the CSV sources. They are not generated by this app.

| Dataset | Rows | Input | Target | Source |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| `Salary_Data.csv` | 30 | `YearsExperience` (years) | `Salary` (original source units) | [Krish Naik, Simple-Linear-Regression](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/krishnaik06/Simple-Linear-Regression/master/Salary_Data.csv) |
| `student_scores.csv` | 25 | `Hours` (study hours) | `Scores` (exam score, %) | [AdiPersonalWorks, Random](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AdiPersonalWorks/Random/master/student_scores%20-%20student_scores.csv) |
| Diabetes (packaged with scikit-learn, no extra CSV) | 442 | `bmi` only (standardized, unitless) | Quantitative disease progression one year after baseline | [scikit-learn documentation](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/datasets/toy_dataset.html#diabetes-dataset), [original diabetes data](https://www4.stat.ncsu.edu/~boos/var.select/diabetes.html) |

The salary CSV does not identify a currency or pay period, so the lab does
not assume dollars, rupees, or an annual salary. The study-hours example and
salary example illustrate approximately linear relationships, not causal
claims or evidence of general predictive accuracy.

Diabetes uses `sklearn.datasets.load_diabetes()` with its default scaling:
BMI is mean-centered and divided by its standard deviation times the square
root of 442. These values are **not raw BMI in kg/m²**. All targets and all
rows are preserved; the other nine features are intentionally excluded to
keep the experiment single-feature. BMI alone has limited predictive power;
a strong fit should not be expected.

All loading is offline, with CSV paths resolved relative to `modules/datasets.py`,
not the working directory. Sample count, noise, and pattern controls apply
only to Synthetic; curated rows are never resampled or perturbed. The usual
training/testing split still applies to every dataset.

## Targeted checks

With the app requirements and the development-only `pytest` package installed:

```text
python -m compileall app.py modules
python -m pytest tests/test_datasets.py tests/test_simulation.py
```

Tests check the published numeric rows, offline/cwd-independent loading,
BMI-only selection, and Streamlit dataset switching, including actual plot
labels, refreshed splits/models/metrics, and restored synthetic controls.
